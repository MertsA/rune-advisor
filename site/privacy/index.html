Privacy policy

Version of 16 November 2026. The policy describes what the service does and what it may come to do; where the two differ today, it says so.

1. Who is responsible

Rune Advisor is operated independently by an individual developer in Washington State, United States, not a company. That person is responsible for the data described here ("we" and "the operator" below).

Contact for anything in this policy: privacy@runeadvisor.com. General questions: support@runeadvisor.com.

2. What is collected, and from where

From the in-game plugin, if you install it and give it a token

Your character's display name and data about your character and your play that the plugin can read from the game. Today that is its game state: skill experience, quest and achievement progress, currencies, and the contents of your own item storage. What the current version sends is listed on what the plugin sends. The plugin sends nothing until you paste a token into it.

The game keeps a plugin's settings for the computer, not for one login. A token saved in the plugin therefore sends every character played in that game client on that computer, yours or anyone else's, until the token is removed from the settings or revoked. On a computer you share, tell the others who play there, or remove the token when you finish.

Later versions may collect more, for example the messages the game itself writes into your chat box (such as "You catch a fish"), your character's position in the game, or a record of a play session: where your character went, what it did, and the experience and items that brought, so the planner can work from what an activity really yields for you. That list is updated before any version that sends more is released, and every plugin version is reviewed by Jagex.

The server keeps the latest state the plugin sent. It may also come to keep the changes the plugin sends as a history of your character's progress; that needs nothing more from the plugin than it sends already.

The game's own definitions. After a game update, the plugin may also send the game's quest and achievement definitions that changed: their names, requirements and descriptions. These describe the game, are the same for every player, and say nothing about you or your character. They are used for every player: to keep the service working after an update, and to develop and improve the planner, of which they may become a part. This does not depend on whether you allow analysis (section 3), and they are kept after the account that sent them is deleted. Definitions sent by one account are used for other players only once they have been checked, by the operator or because enough other accounts sent exactly the same.

Chat between players is never collected. Reading the game's own messages needs the same plugin permission as reading chat, because the game delivers both through the chat box; if a later version asks for that permission, messages written to or by any player, you included, are not sent or stored. The plugin also does not collect other players' data, your Jagex account's login details, or anything about you outside the game.

From Jagex's public services

While your plugin is connected, the service may use your character's name to ask Jagex's public services (such as the hiscores and the Adventurer's Log) for the information about your character that you have chosen to make public in your game settings, and keep it with your character's data. What you have set to private in the game is not available to us this way. The service does not do this yet.

When you make an account

An account is a random key: there is no email address, name or password. We store a hash of the key, a random account id, and when the account was made and last used. For each token: a hash of it, its first six characters, the label you typed, what it may do, when it was made and last used, and which of your characters it has posted for, so that you can tell your tokens apart. For each character: whether, and since when, you allow analysis (section 3). The internet (IP) address an account was made from may be stored, to limit how many accounts can be made from one place.

Making an account runs a check that you are not a bot, provided by Cloudflare (Turnstile). For it your browser contacts Cloudflare, which sees your internet address and technical details of your browser, and we send Cloudflare the address your request came from so that it can confirm the check. Cloudflare uses this to tell people from bots and to improve that detection, not to identify you. It happens only when you make an account: not when you open one, and never from the plugin.

If you send a report from the planner

The planner may let you report a problem with its advice (a planned feature, not built yet). A report holds what you write and which account and character it is about. Only if you choose to allow it with that report, the operator may look at that character's stored data, as it was when you sent the report, to investigate it. Nothing from your data is published: fixes are tested with made-up data, and public notes refer to a report by its number only. What you write in a report is kept (see section 6), so leave out anything personal.

When you write to us

Your email address and whatever you write. To act on a request about an account we may ask you to show that it is yours, for example with one of its tokens.

From every request to the site or the API

The server logs the internet (IP) address the request came from, the time, the request, the response status and size, the referring page and the browser or client name. Keys and tokens are not logged, and neither is the content of what the plugin posts.

Cookies and usage statistics

The site sets no cookies today, and loads nothing from other companies' servers except the bot check when you make an account (above); the account page keeps your key in the page's memory only. We may measure how the site and the service are used (for example unique visitors, or how many players are connected at once), and may use cookies or similar means for that. Such statistics are kept in a form that does not identify you. There is no advertising.

3. What it is used for

To do what the tool is for: to let your planner, or a planner you gave a token to, read your character's data and plan from it, which can include combining what the plugin sent with what Jagex's public services say about the same character; to keep the service running and secure; to understand in aggregate how the service is used and improve it; and to carry out deletions. It is not sold and not shared for advertising.

Analysis to improve the planner, only if you allow it

You can allow a character's data to be analysed to improve the planner for everyone: for example to learn from many players' data how much experience an activity gives, how fast a training method really is, or when the game resets a daily activity. This is optional and off unless you turn it on. The planner works the same for you either way, and allowing it does not make the plugin send anything more or the server keep anything for longer.

The choice is made for each character. You make it, and can change it at any time, in the plugin's settings or on the account page. It covers that character's data whenever it was sent: once you allow it, data already stored may be analysed; once you stop allowing it, no later analysis uses any of it, old or new.

An analysis looks at play, not at players. It reads the data without character names or account details, and only from the characters that allow analysis at that moment. It keeps nothing but its results: the data it read is not kept anywhere outside the service once the analysis is done. Where an analysis runs may change over time: on the service's servers, or on other equipment the operator uses for it, including the operator's own. Wherever it runs, it is in the United States, only the operator and the service providers in section 4 have access, and what this section says still holds.

The results are public. A result is a statement about the game: what an activity gives, how a mechanic works, how fast a method is, or a way of playing that the data showed to work. Results go into the planner and its public source code, and they stay there after you stop allowing analysis or delete your data. A result never names or describes a player: no character name, no account, nothing about what a character owns or has achieved, and nothing about when or how much anyone plays. It does not say whose data it came from or how many characters'.

A result is usually drawn from many characters, but it can come from a few, or from one. If you allow analysis and you play in a way nobody else does, that way of playing may end up described in the planner for everyone, without saying where it came from. If you would rather keep it to yourself, do not allow analysis for that character.

An analysis may also produce statistics about players as a group, such as the share of characters above some total level. These too are drawn only from characters that allow analysis, and may be published. They are published only for groups large enough that nothing can be learned from them about any one player, and never to single out a small group or one whose members could be recognised, such as the owners of a rare item. Of a group too small for that, nothing is published beyond how many characters it holds. Simple counts of how the service is used, such as how many characters allow analysis, are usage statistics (section 2) and do not depend on this choice.

Where UK or EU data protection law applies, these are the legal grounds we rely on:

  • Providing the service you asked for, under the terms you accept by making an account and giving your plugin a token: storing what your plugin sends, handing it to your planner, keeping your account and tokens, looking up your character's public data when you have turned that on, and handling a report you send from the planner.
  • Our legitimate interests in keeping the service secure and working and in knowing how it is used: server logs, limits on how quickly accounts can be made and requests sent, the bot check when an account is made, backups, records of deletions, and usage statistics that do not identify you. You can object to this (section 8).
  • Legal obligations: answering a request you make about your data, and keeping a record that we did.
  • Your consent: analysing what your plugin sends to improve the planner (above). Nothing else relies on your consent, and withdrawing it is as easy as giving it. If a later feature needs consent, it will ask first and you will be able to withdraw it. You do not have to give us any data: without the plugin's data the planner works from public sources only. Nothing here is used to make decisions about you automatically.

4. Who can see it

  • You, and anyone you give a token to (for example a group mate, with a read token limited to the characters you choose).
  • The operator, who runs the service.
  • Service providers the operator uses to run the service, such as the company that hosts the server and Cloudflare for the bot check, only as far as running the service requires.

Game data read through the plugin belongs to Jagex Ltd under its terms for plugins; it is used only for this tool's function and is deleted when you ask.

5. Where it is

On servers in the United States, where the operator and the hosting company are. Your plugin and your browser send data there directly. An analysis you allowed (section 3) may read the data from elsewhere in the United States, on other equipment the operator uses for it.

Backups may also be kept with a storage provider in the United States or the European Union. They are encrypted before they leave the server, and the key that opens them is not kept with them.

If you are in the UK or the EU, this means your data is held in a country that neither has found to protect personal data to the same standard as its own law. Their arrangements with the United States cover only companies that have joined the Data Privacy Framework, which this service, run by an individual, has not. Authorities in the United States could require access to data held there under US law.

What protects your data is therefore what this policy describes, not a law of the country it is in: little is collected, there is no name or email address with it, it is encrypted on the way, backups are encrypted, and you can delete it at any time. Game data that reaches us through the plugin is also covered by Jagex's terms for plugin developers, which include standard data protection clauses for developers outside the UK and the EU.

6. How long it is kept

What Kept
A character's data Until you delete the character or the account, or the account expires. If the server keeps a history of a character's progress, entries older than one year are dropped.
An account and its tokens Until you delete it, or one year after it was last used (a plugin posting or a planner reading), when it is deleted automatically with all its characters.
Records of deletions When you delete an account or a character, or revoke a token, a record that it happened is kept for about 90 days: random ids and hashes, never a character name or a token. It lets a plugin still holding a revoked token be told to stop, and makes sure a restore from a backup cannot bring deleted data back. These records are identifiers of our own, not data from the game.
Reports sent from the planner The text of a report is kept, because it stays useful for later work on the planner, until you ask us to delete it: write to us with the report's number. Its link to your account is removed when the account or the character is deleted. A report holds no copy of character data: it points at the character's stored data, which is kept and deleted as the first row says.
Emails you send us Kept for as long as it takes to deal with your request and to be able to show afterwards that we did. If you ask for a copy of your data, we send it and do not keep the copy with the correspondence.
Server logs Up to 30 days with the internet address. After that the address is removed; what is left of a log line no longer identifies anyone and, like statistics drawn from the logs, may be kept for longer.
Backups Backups are encrypted. Backups that contain character data are kept for up to 14 days, so a deleted character's data is gone from every copy within that time. Backups of account records only (the hashes and ids above, no character data or names) are kept for up to 90 days. Deleted data is deleted again if a backup is ever restored. One exception: a restore can only repeat the deletions it has a record of. If the record of your deletion is itself lost before any backup has captured it, restoring a backup would bring the deleted account or character back, and you would need to delete it again.

7. How it is protected

  • All traffic to the site and the API is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Keys and tokens are stored only as hashes. A token for the plugin cannot read anything back.
  • Backups are encrypted.
  • Access to the server is limited to the operator and the service providers in section 4, and stored character data is accessed only when running the service requires it.

8. Your choices

  • See your data. A read token returns everything stored for a character; the account page shows what is stored about the account and its tokens.
  • Delete it. On the account page, with your key: one character, or the whole account. This takes effect at once on the live service; see section 6 for what remains for a time.
  • Allow or stop allowing analysis, character by character. In the plugin's settings, or on the account page with your key or the plugin's token. It is off unless you turn it on (section 3).
  • Stop sending. Remove the token from the plugin's settings, revoke it, or uninstall the plugin.
  • If you lost your key. The token in your plugin still works for one thing: paste it into the account page in place of the key and you can see and delete what that plugin posted (the plugin's settings can show its token again). Without the key or a token we cannot tell which account is yours; stop the plugin and the account is deleted after a year without use.

Your rights

Where UK or EU data protection law applies, you have the right to:

  • see the data held about you and get a copy of it (a read token returns a character's data in a form another program can read; the account page shows the rest; or ask us);
  • have it corrected (a character's data is whatever its plugin last sent, so it corrects itself at the next post; tell us if something else is wrong);
  • have it deleted;
  • object to what we do on the ground of our legitimate interests (section 3), or ask us to restrict it while a question about it is settled;
  • complain (below).

Most of this you can do yourself on the account page. For anything else write to privacy@runeadvisor.com. We answer within one month; the law allows up to two more for a difficult request, and we would tell you within the first month if we needed them.

Showing that the data is yours. An account has no name or email address, so we can act on an account or a character only when you show that it is yours, with the account key or one of its tokens (the plugin's settings can show its token again). A character's name alone is not proof: anyone can type one, and names change hands in the game. If you can show neither, we are usually unable to tell which data is yours and so unable to act on the request; the account is deleted a year after it was last used in any case. Server logs hold an internet address and a time, and we cannot normally tell whose a line is.

Complaints. Tell us first at privacy@runeadvisor.com: we acknowledge a complaint within 30 days and answer it without undue delay. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live: in the UK the Information Commissioner's Office, in the EU your national authority.

9. Children

The service is not directed at children. You must be at least 13 years old to use it, and if you are under 18 you need your parent's or guardian's permission to agree to the terms, and their permission before you allow analysis (section 3). We do not ask for anyone's age and do not knowingly collect data from a child under 13; a parent or guardian who believes we have can write to privacy@runeadvisor.com and we will delete it.
