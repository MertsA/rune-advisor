Accounts, keys and tokens

There is no login. An account is a random key, and the key hands out tokens for the plugin and the planner. No email address or name is asked for or stored.

The account key

  • Made once, on the account page, and shown once. It starts with raa_. The server keeps only a hash of it, so nobody can look it up for you later.
  • The key manages the account: it makes and revokes tokens and deletes data. It does not post or read character data itself.
  • If you lose the key it cannot be recovered. Your tokens keep working, but you can no longer manage them. The token in your plugin can still delete what it posted: get it back with "Show the saved token" in the plugin's settings and paste it into the account page in place of the key. Then make a new account and give the plugin a token from it; what is left of the old account expires.
  • Keep it like a password. Anyone who has it can delete the account or make a token that reads your characters.

  • The site shows a token once. Give each one a label ("plugin", "laptop planner") so you can tell them apart later.
  • The plugin can show its token again: "Show the saved token" in the plugin's settings puts it back in the field for half a minute so you can copy it. That is the way to get a token back if you need it, for example to prove an account is yours after losing its key.
  • For a group: make a read token limited to one character and give it to your group mate. They see that character and nothing else of the account.
  • Revoking a token takes effect at once. A plugin holding a revoked token is told so, forgets it and stops posting.

Limits and expiry

  • Up to 10 characters and 20 live tokens per account.
  • A few new accounts per internet address per day.
  • An account nobody uses for a year is deleted, with its tokens and every character's state. "Use" means a plugin posting or a planner reading; opening the account page does not count.

Deleting

On the account page, with the key: delete one character's state, or the whole account with every token and character. Deletion from the live service is immediate. What may remain for a time afterwards (backups, a record of the deletion) is listed in the privacy policy.
